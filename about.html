About Brightwell Dental

A Neighborhood Practice Since 2009

Dr. Rachel Moore opened the first Brightwell office with two chairs and one hygienist. Most of our first patients still come to us, and many now bring their kids.

Today we run two offices in Denver with four dentists. We still keep appointments unhurried, explain every option in plain words and give you the price before we start.
