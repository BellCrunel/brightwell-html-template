Dental Implants

An implant is a small titanium post that replaces the root of a missing tooth. After it heals, we attach a crown that looks and works like your own tooth.

Implants don't need the neighbouring teeth to be filed down like a bridge does, and they keep the jaw bone from shrinking. Most healthy adults are good candidates. We check bone and gums with a 3D scan at the first visit.

How It Works

1

Consultation and 3D Scan

We look at the bone, plan the position and give you a written quote.

2

Placing the Implant

A one-hour visit under local anesthetic. Most patients go back to work the next day.

3

Healing

The implant fuses with the bone over three to four months. You wear a temporary tooth meanwhile.

4

Final Crown

We scan, make and fit a ceramic crown matched to your other teeth.

Aftercare

  • Soft foods for the first week
  • Brush and floss around the implant as usual
  • Checkups every six months

Questions About Implants

The area is numb during the procedure. Afterwards most people take regular painkillers for two or three days.

With good home care the implant can last for decades. The crown may need replacing after 15 years or so.

Many plans pay part of the crown. We check your benefits and offer payment plans for the rest.
